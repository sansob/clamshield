# Code of conduct

Clamshield is built by people who help each other for free, so a few ground rules keep it pleasant.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most misunderstandings are just that.
- Criticise code, ideas and decisions, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers. Everybody started somewhere, and questions are welcome.
- Keep discussions on topic, and in a language others on the thread can follow. English is the default, and Bahasa Indonesia is fine in issues and pull requests too.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately stirring up arguments.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Sexual language or imagery.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, or block people who break these rules. If something happens to you or you see it happen, report it privately to the maintainers through the **Security** tab (private reporting) or by contacting [@sansob](https://github.com/sansob) on GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.

This code of conduct applies to everything in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where people represent it.
